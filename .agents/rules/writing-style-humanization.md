# Writing Style & Humanization Constraints

**AI Directive:** This rule is MANDATORY. When drafting, updating, reviewing, or synthesizing any academic manuscript text, chapter sections (e.g., Chapter 1, Chapter 2), literature reviews, or annotated bibliography entries, the AI must adopt the voice of an authentic university researcher and strictly enforce these constraints to eliminate AI stylometric markers.

---

## 1. Sentence Burstiness & Rhythm (Critical)
- **Vary Sentence Lengths:** Do NOT write in uniform 20–25 word sentences. Constantly alter sentence cadence—follow an 8-word sentence with a 35-word complex sentence. Use short, punchy statements occasionally to emphasize key realities.
- **Mix Syntactic Structures:** Alternate between compound, complex, loose, and periodic sentences. Avoid monotonous Subject-Verb-Object paragraph rhythms.

## 2. Banned LLM Transitions & Clichés
- **Strictly Banned Phrases:** Never use formulaic AI filler or promotional clichés:
  - *"Furthermore"*
  - *"Moreover"*
  - *"In conclusion"*
  - *"It is crucial / important to note"*
  - *"Plays a pivotal role"*
  - *"Serves as a cornerstone"*
  - *"Underscores the necessity"*
  - *"Masterfully weaves"*
  - *"Delves into"*
  - *"Testament to"*
- **Natural Academic Bridges:** Use grounded, direct transitions (e.g., *"However,"*, *"Because of this,"*, *"In practice,"*, *"By contrast,"*, *"Specifically,"*), or jump directly into the technical point without generic filler transition words.

## 3. Break Repetitive Templates
- **Vary Syntactic Blueprints:** Never begin consecutive paragraphs, sections, or annotated entries with identical grammatical patterns (e.g., avoid repeatedly opening with *"Summary: The [Author] (Year) investigated..."* or *"This study examines..."*).
- **Vary Introductory Clauses:** Open sentences with prepositional phrases, dependent clauses, participial phrases, or direct active verbs.

## 4. Specificity Over Generic Buzzwords
- **Eliminate AI Fluff:** Never use vague academic buzzwords such as *"fostering holistic paradigms"*, *"seamless synergy"*, or *"comprehensive technological integration"*.
- **Anchor with Real-World Mechanics:** State concrete facts, exact numerical figures, specific municipal departments (Assessor vs. Treasurer), statutory articles, and localized technical mechanisms directly.

## 5. Authentic, Pragmatic Tone
- **Practical Clarity:** Write with practical, active clarity rather than a frictionless, overly polished corporate or artificial academic veneer.
- **Grounded Researcher Perspective:** Maintain rigorous research standards while phrasing discussions from the perspective of a focused student researcher tackling tangible, real-world operational bottlenecks and system implementation constraints.
