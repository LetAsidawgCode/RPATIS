## 1. Project Identity & Metadata
- **Project Title:** Needs Assessment for a Real Property Assessment Tracking and Inter-Office Synchronization System: Resolving Disconnected Records and Workflow Delays in Municipal Assessor and Treasurer Offices in Pasacao
- **Project Proponents:**
  - Rizalina A. Ortega
  - John Kenneth P. Calubad
  - Christian Louise O. Asido
- **Project Type:** Undergraduate Thesis / Capstone Project (Quantitative Research)
- **Degree Program & Academic Department:** Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT), School of Computer and Information Sciences
- **Target Institution / Organization / Community:** Municipal Assessor's and Treasurer's Offices in the municipalities of Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando, with the community of Pasacao serving as the primary benchmark and interview site for the comparative evaluation (in partnership with University of Nueva Caceres)
- **Research Duration / Academic Year:** July 2026 – December 2026 (Academic Year 2026–2027)

## 2. Problem Domain & Urgency
- **Problem Domain:** Local Government Unit (LGU) Real Property Tax (RPT) Administration, Inter-Office Data Synchronization, Municipal E-Governance, Assessment Workflow Digitization
- **Macro / Regulatory Drivers & Institutional Agendas:**
  - *United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:* SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure) and SDG 16 (Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions)
  - *UNC Institutional Research Agenda:* Digital Innovation and Public Service Transformation / Public Systems Modernization
  - *Statutory & Data Protection Mandates:* Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)
- **Local Bottleneck / Operational Failure:**
  - LGUs in Camarines Sur (Canaman, Magarao, San Fernando, and Pasacao) rely on completely disconnected workflows between the Municipal Assessor's Office and the Municipal Treasurer's Office — tracking tax payments and cross-checking assessment rolls requires tedious manual effort.
  - Decades-old legacy records (dating back to 1949) remain in physical form, creating major migration and reconciliation overhead.
  - Even in Pasacao — which already uses a localized eRPT system — a Transfer of Assessment still takes over 7 days to fully process due to disconnected workflow steps.
  - In heavily manual communities like Canaman, assessing a new building requires days of preparation followed by 7–14 days of waiting for physical, paper-based approvals to be routed to the Provincial Capitol in Pili.
  - Manual cross-checking bottlenecks between the two offices cause document processing latency, record reconciliation delays, and inter-office communication lags.

## 3. The Proposed Technical Intervention ("The Proposed System")
- **Standard Project Referent:** Always refer to the project strictly as **"the proposed system"** in all formal manuscript drafts.
- **System Name (Product):** Real Property Assessment Tracking and Inter-Office Synchronization System
- **Core Technological Stack & 3-Tier Web Architecture:**
  - *Tier 1 (Presentation / Frontend):* Web browser interfaces for Assessor evaluation screens, Treasurer tax payment validation views, and document tracking monitors.
  - *Tier 2 (Application / Business Logic / Backend):* Handles assessment data routing, user access permissions, and departmental data integration logic linking property valuations to tax collection records.
  - *Tier 3 (Data / Database Tier):* Centralized relational database storing synchronized property records, active assessment statuses, and transaction logs.
- **Core Functional Modules:**
  1. *Needs Assessment & Comparative Evaluation Module:* Documents operational frictions and system requirements across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando.
  2. *Assessment Request Routing & Document Tracking Module:* Bridges municipal-treasury data silos and automates assessment document routing with real-time status monitoring.
  3. *Tax Payment Synchronization Module:* Eliminates manual cross-checking between the Assessor's and Treasurer's offices by synchronizing property assessment records with tax collection data.
  4. *Administrative Workflow Panel:* Allows personnel to monitor assessment statuses in real-time and manage inter-office data synchronization views.
- **Research Design:** Mixed-Methods Comparative Evaluation Design combining quantitative workflow time-motion metrics with qualitative survey evaluations.
- **Software Development Model:** Agile Development Model (iterative two-week development sprints).

## 4. Specific Objectives & Evaluation Framework
- **Primary Objective:** Determine the operational and technical feasibility of designing and developing a system that addresses the disconnected inter-office workflows and manual record-keeping bottlenecks in the Municipal Assessor's and Treasurer's Offices.
- **Specific Objectives:**
  1. Conduct a needs assessment and comparative evaluation of local municipal workflows between Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando to document specific operational frictions and system requirements.
  2. Develop a web-based Real Property Assessment Tracking and Inter-Office Synchronization System to bridge municipal-treasury data silos and automate assessment document routing.
  3. Conceptualize and specify an integrated inter-office web portal framework that facilitates end-to-end document tracking, allowing personnel to monitor assessment statuses in real-time and eliminating the need for manual cross-checking between departments.
- **Conceptual & Methodological Framework:**
  - *Conceptual Framework:* Input-Process-Output (IPO) Model (Input: stakeholder requirements, workflow latencies, and baseline data from target LGUs; Process: Agile sprints, 3-tier architecture development, system design diagrams (Use Case, DFD, ERD); Output: deployed synchronization platform evaluated under ISO/IEC 25010).
  - *Theoretical Foundation:* ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Requirements and Evaluation.
  - *Software Development Model:* Agile Development Model (iterative sprints).
- **Variables & Evaluation Standards:**
  - *Independent Variable:* Implementation of the Real Property Assessment Tracking and Inter-Office Synchronization System (replacing fragmented manual ledgers and disconnected departmental files).
  - *Dependent Variables (Quantitative):*
    - *Document Processing Latency:* Average time (in days or hours) required to process assessment transactions, such as a Transfer of Assessment.
    - *Record Reconciliation Time:* Time required by staff to cross-check real-time tax payments and verify assessment rolls between the Assessor's and Treasurer's offices.
    - *ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Scores:* Numerical ratings assessing Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Usability, Security, and Reliability.
  - *Qualitative Parameters:* Staff feedback on inter-office workflow ease, document tracking visibility, and operational acceptability in bridging data silos.
- **Statistical Analysis Methods:**
  - *Frequency Count and Percentage:* To summarize demographic profiles and departmental roles of participating municipal officers and administrative staff.
  - *Weighted Mean:* To compute and interpret user evaluation scores from the ISO/IEC 25010 evaluation survey (5-point Likert scale: 1 = Poor to 5 = Excellent).
  - *Comparative Mean Analysis:* To calculate the percentage reduction and statistical difference in document processing and cross-checking times between the baseline processes and the proposed synchronization system.
- **Stakeholder Beneficiaries:**
  - Municipal Assessor's Office Staff (Canaman, Magarao, San Fernando, Pasacao)
  - Municipal Treasurer's Office Staff
  - Property Owners and Local Residents
  - Local Government Units (LGUs) of Camarines Sur
  - Future IT Researchers

## 5. Scope Boundaries & Delimitations
- **Included Scope:**
  - Comparative evaluation across Pasacao (primary benchmark), Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando.
  - Covers inter-office assessment tracking, document routing, tax payment synchronization, and administrative workflow management.
  - Involves pilot deployment with staff from the Municipal Assessor's and Treasurer's Offices.
  - Baseline comparison: manual operations (Canaman) vs. existing localized eRPT (Pasacao) vs. proposed system.
- **Procedural Steps:**
  - *Baseline Assessment:* Document current processing latencies (e.g., 7-day transfer delay in Pasacao, 7–14 days in Canaman) and manual cross-checking bottlenecks.
  - *System Engineering:* Design system diagrams (Use Case, DFD, ERD) and develop the integrated tracking and synchronization platform.
  - *Simulated Trial:* Test data flow, document routing, and inter-office synchronization in a controlled local environment using mock assessment files.
  - *Pilot Deployment:* Conduct field evaluations with municipal staff from both offices under realistic conditions.
- **Explicit Delimitations:**
  - Restricted to assessment tracking and inter-office synchronization; does not include electronic payment collection or fund gateway processing.
  - Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) compliance: actual property records replaced with mock/masked dummy data during development; actual data encrypted with RBAC controls during field deployment.
  - Parallel run phase: digital tracking runs alongside existing municipal workflows during evaluation to ensure data safety and verify platform stability.
