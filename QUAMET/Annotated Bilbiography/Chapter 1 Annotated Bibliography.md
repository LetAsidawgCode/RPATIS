- **Relevance:** This regulatory issuance establishes the primary statutory turnaround benchmark used in the proposed system's needs assessment by defining maximum processing limits against which manual municipal delays—such as the 7-to-14 day assessment latency in Canaman and Pasacao—are evaluated. The proposed system addresses this regulatory requirement by conducting an empirical needs assessment to quantify current processing bottlenecks and determining the technical feasibility of developing an automated document routing and tracking web portal.

### 1. Enhanced Tax Revenue Assessment and Collection System Through the Lens of the Municipal Treasurers and Assessors
**Norberto Arrocena Chinilla and Glenne Berja Lagura. 2025. Enhanced Tax Revenue Assessment and Collection System Through the Lens of the Municipal Treasurers and Assessors. International Journal for Multidisciplinary Research (IJFMR) 7, 4 (July–August 2025), 1–42.**
