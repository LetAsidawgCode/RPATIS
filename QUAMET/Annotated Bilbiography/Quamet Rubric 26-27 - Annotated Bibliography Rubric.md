## 2. Category Definitions

* **Related Literature:** Books, articles, and whitepapers that discuss the theoretical concepts, frameworks, algorithms, or domain knowledge necessary to understand the project.
* **Related Studies:** Peer-reviewed journals and conference papers detailing prior research, experiments, and methodologies.  
  * *Core Question:* What have other researchers discovered or proven about this topic?
* **Related Systems:** Existing software, applications, platforms, or prototypes (both commercial and academic) that are similar to the proposed project.  
  * *Core Question:* What technologies already exist, and what are their limitations?

## 3. Example Reference Entry (ACM Format)

### Citation
> [1] John Doe and Jane Smith. 2025. EduTech: A Smart LMS for Rural Areas. *ACM Trans. Comput. Educ.* 25, 4, Article 11 (October 2025), 15 pages. `https://doi.org/10.1145/1234567.8901234`

### Annotation Structure
> **Annotation:** This paper presents EduTech, an existing learning management system designed for low-bandwidth environments. The authors detail the system's offline-sync architecture and evaluate its usability among 500 students. While the system excels in data compression, the authors admit it lacks real-time collaborative features. This system is highly relevant to my proposed project; I will adopt a similar offline-first database structure, but my system will improve upon EduTech by introducing a lightweight, text-based collaborative module that operates on intermittent connections.
