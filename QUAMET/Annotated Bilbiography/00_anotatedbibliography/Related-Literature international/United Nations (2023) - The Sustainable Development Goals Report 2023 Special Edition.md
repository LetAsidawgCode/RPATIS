# United Nations (2023) - The Sustainable Development Goals Report 2023: Special Edition

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**United Nations. 2023. The Sustainable Development Goals Report 2023: Special Edition. United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs, New York, NY. https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/report/2023/**

- **Summary:** The United Nations (2023) established the global progress and monitoring framework for the 2030 Agenda, defining operational benchmarks under Goal 9 (Target 9.1: resilient technological infrastructure) and Goal 16 (Target 16.6: developing effective, accountable, and transparent institutions at all levels), while documenting that fragmented record-keeping and administrative opacity continue to obstruct local public service modernization.

- **Relevance:** This global policy framework serves as the macro-institutional foundation for the proposed system's administrative tracking dashboard and inter-office synchronization pipeline, directly aligning municipal modernization with SDG 9 and SDG 16.6 targets. However, the United Nations report articulates high-level governance objectives without providing concrete software engineering specifications or data synchronization protocols for municipal revenue authorities. The proposed system operationalizes these global targets into a functional web application that automates assessment tracking and synchronizes property records between assessor and treasurer offices.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Goal 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure (Target 9.1, pp. 24–25):_ Emphasizes developing quality, reliable, sustainable, and resilient infrastructure to support economic development and human well-being.
  - _Goal 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions (Target 16.6 & 16.7, pp. 38–39):_ Calls for developing effective, accountable, and transparent institutions and responsive public decision-making at all administrative levels.
