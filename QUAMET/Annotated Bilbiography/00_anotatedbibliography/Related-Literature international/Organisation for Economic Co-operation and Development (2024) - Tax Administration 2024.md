# Tax Administration 2024: Comparative Information on OECD and Other Advanced and Emerging Economies

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. 2024. Tax Administration 2024: Comparative Information on OECD and Other Advanced and Emerging Economies. OECD Publishing, Paris. https://doi.org/10.1787/2d5fba9c-en**

- **Summary:** The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (2024) analyzed institutional structures and digital transformation benchmarks across 58 tax administrations worldwide, demonstrating that end-to-end electronic data exchange, real-time transaction tracking, and integrated administrative registries significantly reduce processing latencies and reconciliation discrepancies.

- **Relevance:** This comparative international report supports the proposed system's evaluation framework by providing empirical benchmarks on the performance gains of system integration, digital document tracking, and real-time ledger synchronization. While the OECD report evaluates digital maturity across national tax administrations with advanced technological infrastructure, it does not address the localized operational barriers of subnational municipal offices that rely on physical document routing and provincial review cycles. The proposed system resolves this context barrier by determining the feasibility of a web-based inter-office synchronization platform tailored to municipal assessor and treasurer workflows in Philippine local government units.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Chapter 3: Digital Transformation and System Interoperability (pp. 64–82):_ Benchmarks automated data exchange, real-time transaction verification, and application integration across revenue authorities.
  - _Chapter 5: Operational Performance and Processing Efficiency (pp. 110–135):_ Provides empirical comparative data showing latency reductions achieved when administrative silos are replaced with unified database architectures.
