# Revenue Administration Handbook: Modernizing Tax and Customs Operations

**Category:** [[Related Literature international]]

**Raúl Félix Junquera-Varela and Cristian Óliver Lucas-Mas (Eds.). 2024. Revenue Administration Handbook. World Bank, Washington, DC. https://doi.org/10.1596/978-1-4648-2053-3**

- **Summary:** Junquera-Varela and Lucas-Mas (2024) compiled the World Bank Revenue Administration Handbook, presenting institutional blueprints and technical strategies for modernizing revenue collection, emphasizing digital integration, automated data matching, and data-driven workflow coordination across administrative departments.

- **Relevance:** This global handbook informs the technical architecture of the proposed system's Tax Payment Synchronization Module by providing best practices for cross-departmental data reconciliation, transaction auditing, and business process automation. While the World Bank handbook outlines high-level technological adoption pathways and maturity models for national tax agencies, it does not provide localized software architectures designed for resource-constrained municipal governments operating decades-old physical property cards alongside local eRPT installations. The proposed system resolves this gap by conducting a local needs assessment across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando to establish the operational and technical feasibility of an automated document routing and payment reconciliation platform.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Chapter 4: Core Operational Processes and Data Matching (pp. 85–112):_ Discusses business process re-engineering and automated reconciliation mechanisms between property valuation ledgers and revenue collection databases.
  - _Chapter 6: Digital Transformation of Tax Administrations (pp. 145–178):_ Outlines architectural roadmaps for deploying service-oriented web systems, relational data management, and interoperable administrative modules.
