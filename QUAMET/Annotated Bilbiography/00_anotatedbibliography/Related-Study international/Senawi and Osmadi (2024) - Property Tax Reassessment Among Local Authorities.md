# Property Tax Reassessment Among Local Authorities: The Implementation and Its Key Challenges

**Category:** [[Related Study international]]

**Senawi, A. and Osmadi, A. 2024. Property tax reassessment among local authorities: the implementation and its key challenges. *Journal of Financial Management of Property and Construction* 29, 1 (July 2023/2024), 152–167. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/JFMPC-04-2022-0022**

- **Summary:** Investigated property tax reassessment practices across local government authorities in West Malaysia by surveying valuation and assessment officers using PLS-SEM structural equation modeling, finding that municipal revaluations suffer from severe operational inertia—with valuation rolls remaining unrevised for up to 35 years—driven by obsolete property software and fragmented departmental records.

- **Relevance:** This study confirms that municipal property taxation stagnates when local authorities lack modernized assessment software and inter-office record coordination. While Senawi and Osmadi diagnosed administrative friction and software deficiencies across municipal councils without developing a software tool, the proposed system resolves these record-keeping deficits by automating assessment workflows and synchronizing property valuation rolls with treasury tax collection ledgers through a centralized platform.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 4 (Results and Discussion), Reassessment Implementation & Challenges, pp. 158–161:_ Documented that local councils operate with outdated valuation lists for up to 35 years due to software deficiencies, manual cross-referencing, and fragmented records.
  - _Section 5 (Conclusion and Recommendations), pp. 163–165:_ Concluded that local authorities require process innovation and computerized property tracking tools to eliminate administrative valuation bottlenecks.
