# The Role of Information in Property Tax Administration

**Category:** [[Related Study international]]

**Aubrey Harvey Chaputula and Caroline Chingamtolo. 2026. The Role of Information in Property Tax Administration. Journal of Real Estate Practice and Education 28, 1 (April 2026). https://doi.org/10.1080/15214842.2026.2644666**

- **Summary:** Chaputula and Chingamtolo (2026) conducted a mixed-methods empirical case study at Mzuzu City Council in Malawi using key informant interviews and taxpayer surveys, demonstrating that structured municipal information systems managing owner identities, land parcels, and building features increase registered properties and revenue mobilization while identifying that disjointed administrative workflows restrict billing throughput.

- **Relevance:** This empirical study supports the proposed system's browser-based evaluation screens and automated routing logic by demonstrating that digital information systems directly expand parcel registration and collection efficiency in resource-constrained municipal governments. However, Chaputula and Chingamtolo observed that municipal ICT tools operated in isolation without real-time inter-departmental synchronization, leaving back-office verification vulnerable to manual record delays. The proposed system resolves these documented operational constraints by deploying an integrated web architecture that links Municipal Assessor property assessment rolls directly with Municipal Treasurer payment ledgers.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Introduction & Information Categorization (pp. 2–5):_ Framework categorizing municipal property tax data into personal owner identity, cadastral parcel boundaries, and building improvements essential for assessment and valuation.
  - _Results & Discussion on Administrative Challenges (pp. 8–14):_ Empirical documentation showing ICT implementation expanded registered taxpayer rolls and revenue collections, while manual inter-office communication gaps and lack of integrated workflows remained the primary administrative bottleneck.
