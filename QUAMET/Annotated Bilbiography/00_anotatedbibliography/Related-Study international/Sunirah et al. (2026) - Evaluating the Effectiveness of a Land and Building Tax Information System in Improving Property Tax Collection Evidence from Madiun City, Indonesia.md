# Evaluating the Effectiveness of a Land and Building Tax Information System in Improving Property Tax Collection: Evidence from Madiun City, Indonesia

**Category:** [[Related Study international]]

**Iroh Sunirah, Kridawati Sadhana, Roos Widjajani, and Ratih Nindita Kirana. 2026. Evaluating the Effectiveness of a Land and Building Tax Information System in Improving Property Tax Collection: Evidence from Madiun City, Indonesia. International Journal of Management and Economics Invention 12, 7 (July 2026), 5844–5859. https://doi.org/10.47191/ijmei/v12i7.09**

- **Summary:** Sunirah et al. (2026) evaluated the municipal Land and Building Tax Service Information System (SIP-PBBKU) across the Regional Revenue Agency (Bapenda), village administrative officers, and taxpayers in Madiun City, Indonesia, revealing an empirical paradox where active digital taxpayers increased from 43,977 in 2023 to 44,987 in 2024 while realized property tax revenue declined from IDR 23.88 billion to IDR 22.99 billion due to disconnected back-office record-keeping and delayed roll reconciliation.

- **Relevance:** This empirical study supports the proposed system's needs assessment and administrative tracking modules by demonstrating that front-end digital portals fail to optimize municipal revenue when back-office assessment rolls and collection ledgers remain disconnected and manually updated. However, Sunirah et al. restricted their work to descriptive policy evaluation without developing an architectural software solution to automate inter-office record synchronization. The proposed system resolves these operational constraints by deploying an integrated web architecture that automates assessment document tracking and synchronizes property valuation rolls directly with treasury tax collection ledgers in real time.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 1 & Abstract (pp. 5844–5845):_ Empirical documentation of Madiun City's tax digitalization paradox, where paying taxpayers grew from 43,977 (2023) to 44,987 (2024) while realized revenue fell from IDR 23.88B to IDR 22.99B due to uncoordinated back-office systems.
  - _Results & Assessment Matrix (pp. 5850–5854):_ Evaluation showing high effectiveness in citizen-facing digital services contrasted against lower effectiveness in administrative record-keeping, billing enforcement, and manual receivables reconciliation, identifying lack of inter-agency data integration as the primary operational bottleneck.
