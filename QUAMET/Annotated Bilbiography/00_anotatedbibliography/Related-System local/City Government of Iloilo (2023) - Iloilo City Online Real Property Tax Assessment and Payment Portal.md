# Iloilo City Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**[City Government of Iloilo. 2023. Iloilo City Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal. Web application. City Government of Iloilo, Iloilo City, Philippines. https://iloilocity.gov.ph/]**

- **Summary:** The City Government of Iloilo (2023) implements the Iloilo City Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal, an electronic municipal service platform that enables registered property owners to retrieve digitized real property tax orders of payment and settle liabilities through integrated electronic payment gateways.

- **Relevance:** This municipal platform validates the practical advantages of digitizing real property tax orders of payment and automating billing retrieval for local taxpayers; however, the portal is designed strictly as a front-facing billing inquiry and collection tool that depends entirely on static assessment rolls, lacking internal workflow synchronization between the City Assessor's Office and the City Treasurer's Office to trace pending valuation protests, ownership transfers, or physical document routing. The proposed system overcomes this architectural gap by incorporating a centralized document routing engine and an administrative tracking panel, enabling personnel from both offices to track assessment workflows end-to-end and automate record reconciliation in real time.

- **Source Reference(s):**
