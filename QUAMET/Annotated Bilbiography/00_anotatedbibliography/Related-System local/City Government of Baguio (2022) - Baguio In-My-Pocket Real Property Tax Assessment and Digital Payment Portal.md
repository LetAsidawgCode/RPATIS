# Baguio In-My-Pocket: Real Property Tax Assessment and Digital Payment Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**[City Government of Baguio. 2022. Baguio In-My-Pocket: Real Property Tax Assessment and Digital Payment Portal. Mobile and web application. City Government of Baguio, Baguio City, Philippines. https://baguioinmypocket.ph/]**

- **Summary:** The City Government of Baguio (2022) deploys Baguio In-My-Pocket, a municipal mobile and web service platform that allows registered taxpayers to retrieve electronic real property tax assessments and settle tax liabilities through integrated digital payment channels.

- **Relevance:** This digital governance system demonstrates that migrating tax assessments to citizen-facing applications significantly reduces physical queuing at municipal treasury counters and accelerates billing delivery; however, the platform operates with an isolated account-to-document architecture and requires taxpayers to physically visit the City Treasury or City Assessor when property records cannot be retrieved or when ownership adjustments occur, indicating an absence of dynamic inter-office synchronization between assessment rolls and collection databases. The proposed system directly resolves this operational limitation by deploying an integrated web platform with real-time assessment request routing, automated tax payment synchronization, and inter-office status tracking dashboards that eliminate manual counter escalations for municipal staff.
