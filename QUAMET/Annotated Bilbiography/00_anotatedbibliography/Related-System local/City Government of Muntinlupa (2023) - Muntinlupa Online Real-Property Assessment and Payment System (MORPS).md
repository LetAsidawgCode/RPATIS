# Muntinlupa Online Real-Property Assessment and Payment System (MORPS)

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**[City Government of Muntinlupa. 2023. Muntinlupa Online Real-Property Assessment and Payment System (MORPS). Web application. City Government of Muntinlupa, Muntinlupa City, Philippines. http://rpt.muntinlupacity.gov.ph/]**

- **Summary:** The City Government of Muntinlupa (2023) implements the Muntinlupa Online Real-Property Assessment and Payment System (MORPS), a municipal web portal that allows property owners to query tax declaration balances and settle real property tax dues through digital payment gateways.

- **Relevance:** This local government platform demonstrates the operational viability of digitizing taxpayer access to real property tax records using Property Index Numbers and Tax Declaration Numbers; however, MORPS operates primarily as a treasury-managed payment interface that remains functionally isolated from the internal appraisal workflows of the City Assessor's Office, requiring property owners to still physically visit the assessor for property reclassifications, transfer endorsements, and assessment record corrections. The proposed system resolves this departmental boundary by integrating an assessment request routing module and an administrative workflow panel, ensuring that property valuation updates and transfer transactions initiate automated document tracking and bidirectional synchronization between the Assessor's and Treasurer's offices.
