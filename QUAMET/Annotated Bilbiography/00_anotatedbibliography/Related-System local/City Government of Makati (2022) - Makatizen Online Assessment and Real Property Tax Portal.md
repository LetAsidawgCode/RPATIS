# Makatizen Online Assessment and Real Property Tax Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**[City Government of Makati. 2022. Makatizen Online Assessment and Real Property Tax Portal. Web application. City Government of Makati, Makati City, Philippines. https://makationlinepayments.com/]**

- **Summary:** The City Government of Makati (2022) deploys the Makatizen Online Assessment and Real Property Tax Portal, a web-based municipal tax portal allowing property owners to search property assessment records using Tax Declaration Numbers, view quarterly tax liabilities, and settle dues electronically.

- **Relevance:** This metropolitan tax portal illustrates the efficiency gains of providing direct digital inquiry into assessment rolls, enabling taxpayers to review valuation balances remotely; however, the platform operates strictly on pre-validated property records with no delinquencies, providing no automated inter-office coordination between the City Assessor's Office and the City Treasurer's Office when property owners need to reconcile historical assessment discrepancies, process boundary adjustments, or track multi-step assessment transfers. The proposed system directly resolves these operational limitations by engineering an inter-office synchronization engine and document tracking portal that links assessment revisions to treasury ledgers in real time, streamlining document routing and reducing reconciliation delays for municipal staff.
