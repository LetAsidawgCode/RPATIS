# Antipolo Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**[City Government of Antipolo. 2022. Antipolo Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal. Web application. City Government of Antipolo, Antipolo City, Philippines. https://online.antipolo.ph/]**

- **Summary:** The City Government of Antipolo (2022) implements the Antipolo Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal, an electronic local government platform that allows property owners to enroll Tax Declaration Numbers, calculate quarterly property tax liabilities, and complete digital payments through integrated payment channels.

- **Relevance:** This municipal platform demonstrates the effectiveness of self-service digital tax computation in speeding up revenue collection and reducing counter transactions for standardized accounts; however, the portal functions as an isolated treasury-side calculator that requires prior manual verification of historical receipts and offers no dynamic workflow tracking for transactions undergoing assessment revisions, property transfers, or inter-office document approvals with the City Assessor's Office. The proposed system addresses these gaps by implementing end-to-end document routing, real-time assessment tracking monitors, and bidirectional data synchronization between municipal assessor and treasurer offices to handle complex transactions without manual counter appointments.
