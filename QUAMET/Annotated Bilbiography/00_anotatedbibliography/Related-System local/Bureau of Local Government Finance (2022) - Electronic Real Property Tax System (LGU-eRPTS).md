# Electronic Real Property Tax System (LGU-eRPTS): Property Assessment and Municipal Revenue Administration System

**Category:** [[Related System local]]

**[Bureau of Local Government Finance. 2022. Electronic Real Property Tax System (LGU-eRPTS): Property Assessment and Municipal Revenue Administration System. Software application. Department of Finance, Manila, Philippines. http://blgf.gov.ph/]**

- **Summary:** The Bureau of Local Government Finance (2022) implements the Electronic Real Property Tax System (LGU-eRPTS), a standardized municipal software suite deployed across Philippine local government units to automate real property appraisal computations, assessment roll generation, and treasury tax collection records.

- **Relevance:** This nationally deployed system serves as the primary operational benchmark for the study, demonstrating that computerizing property records improves valuation consistency and standardizes tax roll preparation; however, as observed in benchmark implementations such as the Municipality of Pasacao, localized eRPT deployments operate with disconnected workflow steps where processing a Transfer of Assessment still requires over seven days due to manual handoffs and the absence of synchronized tracking between the Assessor's Office and the Treasurer's Office. The proposed system directly resolves these delays by implementing automated assessment request routing, real-time document status monitors, and inter-office payment synchronization to eliminate manual cross-checking and bridge municipal-treasury data silos.
