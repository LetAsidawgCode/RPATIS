# Real Property Tax Collection in Sta. Josefa, Agusan Del Sur, Philippines: An Exploratory Research Analysis

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Fuentes, M.Q. and Salapa, A.C. 2023. Real Property Tax Collection in Sta. Josefa, Agusan Del Sur, Philippines: An Exploratory Research Analysis. *East Asian Journal of Multidisciplinary Research* 2, 11 (Nov. 2023), 4513–4518. DOI: https://doi.org/10.55927/eajmr.v2i11.6542**

- **Summary:** Fuentes and Salapa (2023) evaluated six years (2017–2022) of municipal property tax records from the Electronic New Government Accounting System and the Real Property Tax Information System in Sta. Josefa, Agusan del Sur, using independent samples t-test analysis ($t = -3.674, p = 0.021$) in JASP, finding that collection delinquency surged from 2.05% to 31.72% when administrative outreach was disrupted.

- **Relevance:** This empirical study confirms that municipal tax performance drops sharply when local offices lack continuous, accessible record verification mechanisms outside physical counter payments. While Fuentes and Salapa evaluated historical delinquency trends without engineering an automated software platform, the proposed system resolves these operational vulnerabilities by synchronizing assessor valuation rolls with treasury payment ledgers through an automated inter-office tracking system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Table 1 (Real Property Tax Collections for CY 2017-2022), p. 4515:_ Reported that property tax collection efficiency dropped to 68.28% and delinquency peaked at Php 2,084,545.96 (31.72%) when manual outreach was restricted.
  - _Independent Samples T-Test & Recommendations, pp. 4517–4518:_ Established a statistically significant correlation ($t = -3.674, p = 0.021$) between administrative intervention and collection performance, recommending automated tracking and electronic payment linkages.
