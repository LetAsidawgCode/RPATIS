# Optimizing Internal Revenue Generation for Enhanced Public Service Delivery in the 5th Class Municipalities in Camarines Norte

**Category:** [[Related Study local]]

**Ternida, A.C. and Azuelo, J.B. 2025. Optimizing Internal Revenue Generation for Enhanced Public Service Delivery in the 5th Class Municipalities in Camarines Norte. *International Journal of Research and Scientific Innovation* 12, 5 (May 2025), 189–199. DOI: https://doi.org/10.51244/IJRSI.2025.120500189**

- **Summary:** Evaluated tax administration across 5th class municipalities in Camarines Norte through a quantitative survey of 350 personnel and taxpayers, finding that while local revenue codes are strongly implemented, outdated tax mapping records and fragmented departmental verification severely restrict real property tax generation.

- **Relevance:** This study confirms that municipal tax performance suffers when assessment mapping and collection records operate across disconnected departmental workflows. While Ternida and Azuelo surveyed administrative compliance barriers without developing an automated tool, the proposed system resolves these record-keeping gaps by synchronizing property assessment records with treasury collection data through an automated inter-office tracking platform.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section IV (Results and Discussion), Real Property Tax Administration, p. 194:_ Noted that manual verification of tax declarations against treasury collection books causes reconciliation backlogs across municipal departments.
  - _Section V (Recommendations), p. 197:_ Recommended the adoption of computerized tracking and inter-office data integration to streamline real property assessment workflows.
