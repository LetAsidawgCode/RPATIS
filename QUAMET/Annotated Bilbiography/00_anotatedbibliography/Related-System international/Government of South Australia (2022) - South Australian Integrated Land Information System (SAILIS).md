# South Australian Integrated Land Information System (SAILIS)

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**[Government of South Australia. 2022. South Australian Integrated Land Information System (SAILIS). Web application. Land Services SA and Department for Infrastructure and Transport, Adelaide, SA, Australia. https://sailis.lssa.com.au/]**

- **Summary:** The Government of South Australia (2022) deploys the South Australian Integrated Land Information System (SAILIS), a state government digital portal that provides real-time access to land titles, digital valuation books, survey plans, and historical property transactions.

- **Relevance:** This comprehensive land information system proves the feasibility of maintaining a single digital source of truth for property parcel histories, title transfers, and statutory valuations across public agencies; however, SAILIS functions as a specialized title and cadastral search platform that charges transactional access fees and requires dedicated legal conveyancing intermediaries, offering no lightweight administrative workflow tools for small municipal councils to track day-to-day tax clearance approvals or resolve local assessment backlogs. The proposed system bridges this gap by implementing an internal, web-based administrative workflow panel that tracks assessment document statuses and synchronizes records directly between municipal assessor and treasurer staff at zero transactional cost.
