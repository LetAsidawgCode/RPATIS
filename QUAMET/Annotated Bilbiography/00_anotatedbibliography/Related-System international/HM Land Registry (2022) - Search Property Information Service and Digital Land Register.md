# Search Property Information Service and Digital Land Register

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**[HM Land Registry. 2022. Search Property Information Service and Digital Land Register. Web application. Her Majesty's Land Registry, London, UK. https://www.gov.uk/search-property-information-land-registry]**

- **Summary:** HM Land Registry (2022) deploys the Search Property Information Service, an official UK government digital platform that provides citizens, conveyancers, and local rating authorities with electronic access to land titles, property boundaries, and registered ownership histories.

- **Relevance:** This national digital registry demonstrates the technical efficiency of centralized property ownership verification in curbing fraudulent transfers and reducing administrative cross-checking; however, the platform operates as an external title recording repository that remains structurally detached from local council valuation and council tax collection databases, requiring local billing authorities to manually query the registry to reconcile property ownership changes. The proposed system resolves this operational boundary by engineering an integrated assessment tracking platform that automates document routing and directly links property ownership transfer workflows to local treasury tax collection ledgers in real time.
