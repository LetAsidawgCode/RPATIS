# City of Cape Town General Valuation Roll and Property Rates Portal

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**[City of Cape Town. 2023. City of Cape Town General Valuation Roll and Property Rates Portal. Web application. City of Cape Town Metropolitan Municipality, Cape Town, South Africa. https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/gv2022/]**

- **Summary:** The City of Cape Town (2023) deploys the General Valuation Roll and Property Rates Portal, a municipal web platform that publishes certified property valuation rolls, provides rate calculation estimates, and manages digital objection submissions for metropolitan property owners.

- **Relevance:** This portal demonstrates how public digital valuation rolls improve transparency and reduce counter inquiries. However, it only publishes static periodic rolls and lacks workflow tracking between valuation and revenue offices. The proposed system resolves this limitation by providing real-time document routing and assessor-treasurer data synchronization to eliminate inter-office processing delays.

  - _[Cape Town Property Rates Policy and Administrative Framework]:_ Describes the four-year general valuation review cycle and supplementary valuation processing.
