# e-LAVS: Electronic Land Administration and Valuation System

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**[National Land Agency of Jamaica. 2022. e-LAVS: Electronic Land Administration and Valuation System. Web application. National Land Agency, Kingston, Jamaica. https://www.nla.gov.jm/]**

- **Summary:** The National Land Agency of Jamaica (2022) deploys e-LAVS (Electronic Land Administration and Valuation System), an integrated government web portal that combines land parcel mapping, statutory property valuation rolls, and property tax assessment certificates within a digitized cadastral framework.

- **Relevance:** This developing-nation e-governance system provides a direct architectural precedent for modernizing property valuation and land taxation in jurisdictions transitioning away from manual record books; however, e-LAVS operates primarily at the national agency level and experiences data synchronization lags when routing valuation revisions to localized parish tax collection offices, where local revenue staff still face manual record reconciliation delays. The proposed system directly resolves this inter-office latency by prioritizing local municipal-level integration, establishing an automated document routing and tax payment synchronization pipeline between the Municipal Assessor's Office and the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
