# NSW Valuation and Land Values Portal

**Category:** [[Related System international]]

**[NSW Valuer General. 2023. NSW Valuation and Land Values Portal. Web application. New South Wales Government, Sydney, NSW, Australia. https://www.valuergeneral.nsw.gov.au/]**

- **Summary:** The NSW Valuer General (2023) deploys the NSW Valuation and Land Values Portal, an official state government web platform that publishes statutory land assessments, provides valuation search tools, and processes digital valuation objections for property owners across New South Wales.

- **Relevance:** This statutory government system demonstrates how a centralized valuation database provides a standardized baseline for municipal property taxation; however, the portal operates on a periodic valuation transmission cycle where the central valuation authority delivers assessment rolls to local councils on a decoupled schedule, lacking real-time, event-driven synchronization with local council rating ledgers when property boundaries are subdivided or when ownership transfers occur mid-cycle. The proposed system directly overcomes this operational barrier by designing a continuous, event-triggered inter-office synchronization pipeline between the Municipal Assessor's Office and the Municipal Treasurer's Office, ensuring that assessment updates instantly reflect in tax collection records without multi-week administrative delays.
