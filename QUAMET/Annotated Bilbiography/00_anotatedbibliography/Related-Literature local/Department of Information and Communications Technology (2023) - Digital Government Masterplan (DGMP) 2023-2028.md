# DICT Digital Government Masterplan (DGMP) 2023–2028: One Digitized Government

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Department of Information and Communications Technology. 2023. Digital Government Masterplan (DGMP) 2023–2028: Streamlining Public Service Delivery Through Integrated Local e-Governance. Republic of the Philippines, Quezon City. https://dict.gov.ph/digital-government-masterplan-2023-2028/**

- **Summary:** The Department of Information and Communications Technology (2023) promulgated the Digital Government Masterplan (DGMP) 2023–2028, establishing the national strategic roadmap for "One Digitized Government" to harmonize public sector ICT infrastructure, minimize manual administrative handoffs, and mandate end-to-end electronic service delivery across local government units.

- **Relevance:** This national strategic masterplan directly establishes the institutional rationale and performance benchmarks for the proposed system's needs assessment and feasibility study. While the DGMP articulates a nationwide mandate for unified digital governance and integrated local public services, lower-class municipalities frequently operate without targeted architectural blueprints to modernize disconnected back-office workflows between assessor and treasurer departments. The proposed system addresses this policy priority by conducting a comparative needs assessment across benchmark municipalities in Camarines Sur to quantify manual administrative delays and establish the technical and operational feasibility of developing an integrated inter-office tracking and synchronization web platform.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Strategic Pillar 1: Digitalizing Government Frontline and Back-Office Operations (pp. 18–26):_ Mandates the elimination of physical document handoffs and the transition to automated electronic routing across subnational government units.
  - _Strategic Pillar 3: Interoperable Local Public Infrastructure (pp. 42–50):_ Sets national milestones for cross-departmental data integration, unified databases, and citizen service delivery tracking.
