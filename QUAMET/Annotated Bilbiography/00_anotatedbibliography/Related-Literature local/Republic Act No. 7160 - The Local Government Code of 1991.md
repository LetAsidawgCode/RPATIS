# Republic Act No. 7160: The Local Government Code of 1991 (Book II, Title II: Real Property Taxation)

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Republic of the Philippines. 1991. Republic Act No. 7160: An Act Providing for a Local Government Code of 1991 (Book II, Title II: Real Property Taxation). Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines, Manila. https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/**

- **Summary:** Republic Act No. 7160 (1991) establishes the statutory framework for local government administration in the Philippines, delineating the appraisal and assessment mandates of the Municipal Assessor under Title II alongside the independent tax collection, receipting, and enforcement duties assigned to the Municipal Treasurer.

- **Relevance:** This statutory code identifies the institutional foundation of the inter-office workflow divide by legally separating property valuation from tax collection, thereby explaining why municipalities like Canaman, Magarao, San Fernando, and Pasacao operate disconnected back-office ledgers. While RA 7160 specifies the administrative responsibilities of municipal assessors and treasurers, it was drafted prior to contemporary web systems and does not specify electronic data-sharing mechanisms between departments. The proposed system resolves this operational disconnect by implementing an integrated tracking and synchronization platform that automates assessment routing and links valuation records with municipal treasury collection files in real time.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Book II, Title II, Chapter 2: Appraisal and Assessment of Real Property (Sections 219–225):_ Details the statutory obligations of assessors in preparing assessment rolls, appraisal schedules, and property tax declarations.
  - _Book II, Title II, Chapter 5: Collection of Real Property Tax (Sections 246–250):_ Prescribes treasury responsibilities in billing, collection enforcement, receipt issuance, and maintaining tax payment ledgers.
