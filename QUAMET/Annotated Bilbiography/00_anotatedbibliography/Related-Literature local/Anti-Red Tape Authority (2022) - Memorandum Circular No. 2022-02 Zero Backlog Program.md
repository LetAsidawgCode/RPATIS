# ARTA Memorandum Circular No. 2022-02: Zero Backlog Program and Prescribed Processing Time

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Anti-Red Tape Authority. 2022. Memorandum Circular No. 2022-02: Compliance on Automatic Approval, Zero-Contact Policy, and Zero Backlog Program for Government Agencies and Local Government Units. Republic of the Philippines, Office of the President, Manila. https://arta.gov.ph/issuances/memorandum-circulars/**

- **Summary:** The Anti-Red Tape Authority (2022) issued Memorandum Circular No. 2022-02 pursuant to Republic Act No. 11032, directing all government agencies and local government units to implement mandatory Zero Backlog programs, enforce the Zero-Contact Policy, and re-engineer operational workflows to ensure government transactions do not exceed statutory processing time limits.

- **Relevance:** This regulatory issuance establishes the primary statutory turnaround benchmark used in the proposed system's needs assessment by defining maximum processing limits against which manual municipal delays—such as the 7-to-14 day assessment latency in Canaman and Pasacao—are evaluated. While ARTA legally mandates zero-backlog compliance and workflow re-engineering, it provides administrative directives rather than technical software architectures to eliminate the physical paper handoffs between municipal assessors and treasurers. The proposed system addresses this regulatory requirement by conducting an empirical needs assessment to quantify current processing bottlenecks and determining the technical feasibility of developing an automated document routing and tracking web portal.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 3: Mandatory Implementation of Zero Backlog Program (pp. 2–3):_ Mandates that local government units audit pending transactions and eliminate administrative backlogs in processing public permits, assessments, and clearances.
  - _Section 4: Prescribed Processing Time and Automatic Approval (pp. 3–5):_ Reiterates strict adherence to statutory service delivery periods (3, 7, and 20 working days) and enforces automated administrative handoffs.
