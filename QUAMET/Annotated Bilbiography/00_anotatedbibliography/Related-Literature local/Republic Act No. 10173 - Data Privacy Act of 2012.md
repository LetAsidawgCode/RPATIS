# Republic Act No. 10173: Data Privacy Act of 2012 and Its Implementing Rules and Regulations

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Republic of the Philippines. 2012. Republic Act No. 10173: An Act Protecting Individual Personal Information in Information and Communications Systems in the Government and the Private Sector (Data Privacy Act of 2012). Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines, Manila. https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2012/08/15/republic-act-no-10173/**

- **Summary:** Republic Act No. 10173 (2012) mandates comprehensive data protection, confidentiality standards, and technical safeguards across all public and private entities processing personal information, holding government agencies accountable for securing citizen identifiers and asset records against unauthorized access.

- **Relevance:** This national statutory act defines the primary legal and data protection compliance requirements for the proposed system, specifically regarding the handling of sensitive property titles, owner tax identification numbers, and municipal valuation rolls. While the statute mandates data minimization and strict security safeguards, manual municipal offices frequently leave physical assessment cards and paper tax rolls unmonitored in open municipal workspaces. The proposed system addresses this regulatory mandate by embedding role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end audit trails, and masked dummy data protocols during development and field testing to ensure strict compliance with statutory privacy mandates.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 20: Security of Personal Information (pp. 8–10):_ Mandates reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, alteration, and unauthorized disclosure.
  - _Section 22: Principle of Accountability (pp. 11–12):_ Requires government entities processing sensitive citizen data to designate compliance officers and maintain auditable processing logs.
