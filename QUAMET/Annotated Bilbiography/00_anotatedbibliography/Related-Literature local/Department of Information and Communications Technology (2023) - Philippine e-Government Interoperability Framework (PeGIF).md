# Philippine e-Government Interoperability Framework (PeGIF)

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Department of Information and Communications Technology. 2023. Philippine e-Government Interoperability Framework (PeGIF): Technical Data and Information Interoperability Standards for Local Government Units. Republic of the Philippines, Quezon City. https://dict.gov.ph/philippine-e-government-interoperability-framework/**

- **Summary:** The Department of Information and Communications Technology (2023) established the Philippine e-Government Interoperability Framework (PeGIF) across technical, data, and organizational domains, defining standardized data exchange schemas, open API communication protocols, and semantic harmonization to eliminate departmental information silos across local government operations.

- **Relevance:** This national interoperability standard justifies the operational necessity of developing the proposed system by demonstrating that municipal departments operating isolated software subsystems violate national data integration guidelines. While DICT defines macro-level technical domains and data exchange standards, it does not provide localized workflow models or database synchronization architectures tailored to the daily operational divide between municipal assessor valuation records and treasury collection ledgers. The proposed system addresses this gap by conducting a localized needs assessment across benchmark municipalities in Camarines Sur to establish the operational necessity and architectural feasibility of an interoperable web platform bridging municipal assessor and treasurer workflows.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Information Interoperability Domain (pp. 14–22):_ Mandates uniform data dictionary definitions, semantic metadata harmonization, and shared data registries across public sector agencies to eliminate disconnected departmental ledgers.
  - _Technical Interoperability Domain (pp. 28–36):_ Recommends service-oriented architectures, RESTful data exchange, and open relational database schemas to ensure reliable system-to-system synchronization in local government units.
