# Republic Act No. 12001: Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act (RPVARA)

**Category:** [[Related Literature local]]

**Republic of the Philippines. 2024. Republic Act No. 12001: An Act Instituting Reforms in Real Property Valuation and Assessment in the Philippines, Reorganizing the Bureau of Local Government Finance, Granting Tax Amnesty on Real Property and Special Levies on Real Property, and Appropriating Funds Therefor (Real Property Valuation and Assessment Reform Act). Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines, Manila. https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2024/06/13/republic-act-no-12001/**

- **Summary:** Republic Act No. 12001 (2024) mandates national reforms in real property valuation and assessment across all local government units, establishing a comprehensive and up-to-date electronic database of real property transactions administered by the Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGF) through the Real Property Information System (RPIS), while directing LGUs to adopt innovative digital technology in local property tax administration to improve revenue generation capacity.

- **Relevance:** This statutory mandate establishes the most direct legal basis for the proposed system's inter-office tracking and synchronization architecture by compelling LGUs to maintain digitized property assessment records and electronically synchronized transaction data accessible to both Assessor and Treasurer offices. While RA 12001 defines national policy directives and mandates the BLGF-administered RPIS as the central electronic database, it does not prescribe localized inter-office workflow routing systems or inter-departmental document tracking dashboards at the municipal level. The proposed system addresses this operational gap by conducting a needs assessment across benchmark municipalities in Camarines Sur to determine how the RPVARA's electronic database mandate can be operationalized through an integrated web-based tracking and synchronization platform bridging the Municipal Assessor's and Treasurer's offices.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 3(e): Purposes (p. 1):_ "Provide a comprehensive and up-to-date electronic database of all real property transactions."
  - _Section 3(h): Purposes (p. 1):_ "Promote the use of innovative digital technology in local and real property tax administration, real property transactions, and other business processes to improve the LGUs' revenue generation capacity."
  - _Section 5(g): Powers and Functions of BLGF (p. 2):_ "Develop and maintain a comprehensive and up-to-date electronic database of real property transactions and prices of materials for buildings, machinery, and other structures."
  - _Section 5(i): Powers and Functions of BLGF (p. 2):_ "Formulate and provide for uniform procedures on the different transactions in the assessor's office, including the transfer, annotation, and issuance of tax declarations."
