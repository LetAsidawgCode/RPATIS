# NPC Circular No. 2022-04: Registration of Personal Data Processing Systems and Governance Standards

**Category:** [[Related Literature local C2]]

**National Privacy Commission. 2022. NPC Circular No. 2022-04: Registration of Personal Data Processing Systems, Notification Regarding Automated Decision-Making or Profiling. Republic of the Philippines, Pasay City. https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04-2.pdf**

- **Summary:** Issued by the National Privacy Commission under Republic Act No. 10173, Circular No. 2022-04 establishes mandatory registration, technical security baselines, and governance safeguards for personal data processing systems operated by government instrumentalities and local government units.

- **Relevance:** This regulatory issuance establishes the statutory data protection and privacy compliance benchmarks for the proposed system's comparative evaluation across Canaman, Magarao, San Fernando, and Pasacao. Within the proposed system, the circular's security mandates directly drive the role-based access control (RBAC) encryption pipelines, user authentication middleware, and encrypted relational schemas engineered into the Administrative Workflow Panel and Assessment Request Routing Module. Unsecured paper ledgers expose sensitive citizen assets. Decades-old assessment rolls dating back to 1949 remain vulnerable to physical theft and unauthorized viewing in open municipal workspaces. While the NPC circular legally enforces data protection thresholds and mandates data protection officer oversight, it functions purely as an administrative compliance instrument; it specifies no software encryption algorithms, relational schema safeguards, or inter-office authentication protocols for municipal assessment workflows. The proposed system bridges this operational vulnerability by embedding role-based access control (RBAC), encrypted relational database storage for taxpayer identification numbers, and immutable transaction audit logs directly into the proposed system.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 5: Mandatory Registration of Government Data Processing Systems (pp. 3–5):_ Classifies municipal administrative databases as critical data processing systems requiring formal registration, security oversight, and appointed data protection officers.
  - _Section 9: Technical Security and Access Control Measures (pp. 7–9):_ Mandates automated transaction logging, strict role-based user authentication, and data minimization protocols to prevent unauthorized viewing or tampering with public records.
