# Philippine e-Government Interoperability Framework (PeGIF)

**Category:** [[Related Literature local C2]]

**Department of Information and Communications Technology. 2023. Philippine e-Government Interoperability Framework (PeGIF): Technical Data and Information Interoperability Standards for Local Government Units. Republic of the Philippines, Quezon City. https://dict.gov.ph/philippine-e-government-interoperability-framework/**

- **Summary:** Formulated across technical, semantic, and organizational governance domains, the Philippine e-Government Interoperability Framework (PeGIF) standardizes inter-agency data exchange protocols, common data dictionaries, and architectural guidelines to dismantle operational silos across subnational government institutions.

- **Relevance:** This regulatory framework provides the primary technical and architectural rationale for the proposed system's inter-office data integration pipeline. Within the proposed system, PeGIF’s semantic and technical interoperability guidelines directly inform the automated foreign-key joins, standardized parcel data dictionaries, and relational table mappings engineered into the Tax Payment Synchronization Module. Administrative silos cripple subnational operations. While PeGIF defines high-level data dictionaries and service-oriented standards, it offers no tangible relational schemas, transaction-matching algorithms, or field-level reconciliation logic to resolve the severe operational divide between municipal assessor valuation files and treasury collection ledgers. The proposed system resolves this architectural gap by executing a comparative needs assessment across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando and deploying a unified web platform that synchronizes assessment values with real-time tax payments through automated foreign-key mapping.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Information Interoperability Domain (pp. 14–22):_ Mandates uniform data dictionary definitions, semantic metadata harmonization, and shared data registries across public sector agencies to eliminate disconnected departmental ledgers.
  - _Technical Interoperability Domain (pp. 28–36):_ Recommends service-oriented architectures, RESTful data exchange, and open relational database schemas to ensure reliable system-to-system synchronization in local government units.
