# DICT Digital Government Masterplan (DGMP) 2023–2028: One Digitized Government

**Category:** [[Related Literature local C2]]

**Department of Information and Communications Technology. 2023. Digital Government Masterplan (DGMP) 2023–2028: Streamlining Public Service Delivery Through Integrated Local e-Governance. Republic of the Philippines, Quezon City. https://dict.gov.ph/digital-government-masterplan-2023-2028/**

- **Summary:** Drafted as the national strategic roadmap for Philippine public sector modernization, the Digital Government Masterplan 2023–2028 directs subnational agencies and local government units to dismantle administrative silos, interconnect fragmented frontline registries, and deploy interoperable digital platforms for public service delivery.

- **Relevance:** This strategic roadmap establishes the policy baseline and national interoperability targets evaluated in the proposed system's needs assessment across Camarines Sur municipalities. Grounded in the DGMP’s integration mandates, the proposed system incorporates an event-triggered inter-office data pipeline and centralized relational property database within the Administrative Workflow Panel to bridge disconnected local registries. Macro-level policy frameworks rarely translate into immediate municipal realities. Although the DGMP outlines ambitious milestones for cross-agency data exchange, it provides high-level administrative visions rather than lightweight architectural blueprints or operational workflows adapted to resource-constrained fourth-class municipalities burdened by manual ledgers dating back to 1949. The proposed system directly overcomes this institutional disconnect by evaluating operational frictions across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando, delivering a targeted synchronization web platform that automates inter-office record verification without requiring costly enterprise overhauls.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Strategic Pillar 1: Digitalizing Government Frontline and Back-Office Operations (pp. 18–26):_ Directs local government units to automate administrative workflows and phase out physical document routing in municipal offices.
  - _Strategic Pillar 3: Interoperable Local Public Infrastructure (pp. 42–50):_ Outlines statutory targets for subnational data integration, centralized citizen registries, and inter-office transactional tracking.
