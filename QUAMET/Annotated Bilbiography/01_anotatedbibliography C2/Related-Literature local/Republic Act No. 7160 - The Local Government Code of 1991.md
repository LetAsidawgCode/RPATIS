# Republic Act No. 7160: The Local Government Code of 1991 (Book II, Title II: Real Property Taxation)

**Category:** [[Related Literature local C2]]

**Republic of the Philippines. 1991. Republic Act No. 7160: An Act Providing for a Local Government Code of 1991 (Book II, Title II: Real Property Taxation). Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines, Manila. https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1991/10/10/republic-act-no-7160/**

- **Summary:** Enacted as the statutory foundation for Philippine local autonomy, Book II, Title II of Republic Act No. 7160 establishes the appraisal, assessment, and real property roll preparation duties of the Municipal Assessor while designating the local treasurer with independent responsibility for property tax collection, enforcement, and billing.

- **Relevance:** This foundational statute establishes the institutional division of labor that creates the operational disconnect evaluated in the proposed system's comparative assessment across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando. Within the proposed system, the distinct statutory mandates defined in Book II, Title II directly govern the automated foreign-key mapping and scheduled/event-driven ledger reconciliation routines linking the Tax Payment Synchronization Module with the Assessment Request Routing Module. Statutory silos divide municipal halls. Under Section 248, the law requires the municipal assessor to furnish the local treasurer with an assessment roll only once a year, on or before December 31. Because of this outdated 1991 batch mechanism, day-to-day property reassessments and ownership transfers trigger severe operational friction, resulting in 7-day transfer delays in Pasacao and up to 14 days of manual paper tracking in Canaman. While RA 7160 comprehensively defines municipal appraisal duties and collection powers, it contains no technical frameworks for digital inter-office communication, shared relational databases, or real-time payment reconciliation. The proposed system directly bridges this statutory void by interconnecting assessor appraisal records with treasury collection rolls through an automated web architecture, replacing slow physical handoffs with real-time database synchronization.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 201 & 248: Real Property Appraisal and Assessment Roll Submission (pp. 92–108):_ Mandates the municipal assessor to appraise all taxable real property at current market value and compile the annual assessment roll for physical delivery to the local treasurer on or before December 31.
  - _Section 247: Collection of Real Property Tax and Deputization (pp. 107–108):_ Vests exclusive authority in the municipal treasurer to collect real property taxes, enforce statutory remedies, and deputize bonded barangay treasurers.
