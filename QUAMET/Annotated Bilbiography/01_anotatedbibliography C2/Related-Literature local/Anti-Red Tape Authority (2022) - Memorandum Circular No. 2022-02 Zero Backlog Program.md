# ARTA Memorandum Circular No. 2022-02: Zero Backlog Program and Prescribed Processing Time

**Category:** [[Related Literature local C2]]

**Anti-Red Tape Authority. 2022. Memorandum Circular No. 2022-02: Compliance on Automatic Approval, Zero-Contact Policy, and Zero Backlog Program for Government Agencies and Local Government Units. Republic of the Philippines, Office of the President, Manila. https://arta.gov.ph/issuances/memorandum-circulars/**

- **Summary:** Operating under Republic Act No. 11032, the Anti-Red Tape Authority (2022) mandated all government entities and local government units to enforce statutory processing windows, eliminate legacy paper backlogs, and implement a strict Zero-Contact Policy across frontline public transactions.

- **Relevance:** This regulatory issuance establishes the statutory turnaround benchmarks for the proposed system's needs assessment and comparative evaluation across Canaman, Magarao, San Fernando, and Pasacao by providing legal criteria to assess document processing latencies. The proposed system embeds these statutory timelines directly into the automated routing queues and browser-based tracking monitors of its Assessment Request Routing and Document Tracking Module to track municipal assessment status automatically. ARTA mandates strict processing turnaround thresholds of 3, 7, and 20 days, but the circular functions purely as a regulatory directive; it provides no software architectures, automated database schemas, or inter-office data pipelines to eliminate manual paper routing between municipal assessors and treasurers. The proposed system resolves this operational gap by translating ARTA’s administrative zero-backlog mandate into an automated web architecture that tracks assessment documents in real time and eliminates physical inter-departmental handoffs.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Section 3: Mandatory Implementation of Zero Backlog Program (pp. 2–3):_ Directs government agencies and LGUs to audit pending transactions and adopt measures eliminating administrative backlogs in processing public transactions.
  - _Section 4: Prescribed Processing Time and Automatic Approval (pp. 3–5):_ Enforces statutory turnaround thresholds (3 working days for simple transactions, 7 for complex transactions, and 20 for highly technical applications) under Republic Act No. 11032.
