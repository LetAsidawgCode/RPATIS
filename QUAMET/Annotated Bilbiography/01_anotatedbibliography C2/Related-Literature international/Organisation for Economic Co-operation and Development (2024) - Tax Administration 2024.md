# Tax Administration 2024: Comparative Information on OECD and Other Advanced and Emerging Economies

**Category:** [[Related Literature international C2]]

**Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. 2024. Tax Administration 2024: Comparative Information on OECD and Other Advanced and Emerging Economies. OECD Publishing, Paris. https://doi.org/10.1787/2d5fba9c-en**

- **Summary:** Covering fifty-eight tax authorities across advanced and emerging economies, this comparative report by the OECD (2024) demonstrates that deploying real-time digital registries, automated inter-agency data exchanges, and unified transactional queues substantially reduces processing latency and eliminates ledger reconciliation errors.

- **Relevance:** This comparative performance survey provides the empirical benchmarks and evaluation rationale for the quantitative metrics measured in the proposed system's comparative evaluation across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando. Drawing from OECD operational indicators, the proposed system defines operational efficiency benchmarks for the real-time tracking monitors, event-driven state machines, and administrative dashboards within the Administrative Workflow Panel. Manual verification cripples frontline municipal service. In heavily paper-reliant municipalities like Canaman, assessing a building requires days of preparation followed by one to two weeks of waiting for physical files to route to the Provincial Capitol. Although the OECD report provides rigorous performance data across 58 jurisdictions, its findings reflect well-funded national administrations equipped with mature IT departments; it fails to investigate the severe technological constraints of subnational municipal offices reliant on physical ledger books dating back to 1949. The proposed system directly overcomes this contextual barrier by developing a lightweight web architecture that automates inter-office document tracking and synchronizes property assessment records with local treasury ledgers in real time.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Chapter 3: Digital Transformation and System Interoperability (pp. 64–82):_ Details international benchmarks for automated data exchange APIs, real-time transaction processing, and integrated taxpayer registries across 58 revenue bodies.
  - _Chapter 5: Operational Performance and Processing Efficiency (pp. 110–135):_ Provides empirical comparative data demonstrating that eliminating departmental silos cuts transaction processing delays and reduces cross-ledger verification discrepancies by more than half.
