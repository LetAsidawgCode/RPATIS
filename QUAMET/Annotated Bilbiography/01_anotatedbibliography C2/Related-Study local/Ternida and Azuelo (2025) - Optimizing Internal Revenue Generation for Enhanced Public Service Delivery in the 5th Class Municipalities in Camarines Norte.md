# Optimizing Internal Revenue Generation for Enhanced Public Service Delivery in the 5th Class Municipalities in Camarines Norte

**Category:** [[Related Study local C2]]

**A. C. Ternida and J. B. Azuelo. 2025. Optimizing Internal Revenue Generation for Enhanced Public Service Delivery in the 5th Class Municipalities in Camarines Norte. International Journal of Research and Scientific Innovation 12, 5 (May 2025), 189–199. https://doi.org/10.51244/IJRSI.2025.120500189**

- **Summary:** In a field investigation of 350 municipal personnel and taxpayers across fifth-class municipalities in Camarines Norte, Ternida and Azuelo (2025) demonstrated that while administrative revenue mandates are formally enforced, manual verification between assessor tax declarations and treasury cashbooks creates persistent reconciliation backlogs that choke local tax collection.
 
- **Relevance:** This empirical study provides direct Bicol regional evidence justifying the operational necessity of the proposed system's comparative evaluation across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando. Investigating resource-constrained 5th class municipalities, Ternida and Azuelo’s findings directly inform the automated foreign-key joins, relational table mapping, and scheduled reconciliation routines built into the Tax Payment Synchronization Module. Paper verification chokes municipal revenue. In small Bicol local government units, treasury clerks spend hours physically matching individual assessment declarations against handwritten tax registers before clearing property transactions. While Ternida and Azuelo thoroughly document these administrative bottlenecks across 350 respondents, their study concludes with policy recommendations; they develop no automated web applications, relational schemas, or document tracking state machines. The proposed system directly resolves this regional operational barrier by deploying an integrated web platform that joins property assessment cards with municipal treasury payment ledgers in real time, eliminating manual cross-referencing delays.

  - _Section V: Recommendations for Digital Governance (p. 197):_ Concludes that fifth-class local government units must implement computerized document tracking and integrated inter-office databases to eliminate administrative bottlenecks.
