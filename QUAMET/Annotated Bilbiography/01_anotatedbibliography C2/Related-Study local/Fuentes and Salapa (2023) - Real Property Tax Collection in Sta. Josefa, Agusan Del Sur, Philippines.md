# Real Property Tax Collection in Sta. Josefa, Agusan Del Sur, Philippines: An Exploratory Research Analysis

**Category:** [[Related Study local C2]]

**M. Q. Fuentes and A. C. Salapa. 2023. Real Property Tax Collection in Sta. Josefa, Agusan Del Sur, Philippines: An Exploratory Research Analysis. East Asian Journal of Multidisciplinary Research 2, 11 (November 2023), 4513–4518. https://doi.org/10.55927/eajmr.v2i11.6542**

- **Summary:** Based on six years of fiscal data (2017–2022) in Sta. Josefa, Agusan del Sur, an independent samples t-test ($t = -3.674$, $p = 0.021$) conducted by Fuentes and Salapa (2023) revealed that tax delinquency escalated from 2.05% to 31.72% when municipal counter interactions and manual tax outreach were disrupted.
 
- **Relevance:** This empirical study provides concrete localized evidence of fiscal fragility for the proposed system's comparative evaluation across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando. Demonstrating that physical counter dependencies cause delinquency to spike past 31%, Fuentes and Salapa’s findings directly justify the event-driven ledger reconciliation routines and centralized relational schema connecting assessor rolls with treasury records in the Tax Payment Synchronization Module. Physical counter dependencies jeopardize revenue. In Philippine rural LGUs, property owners routinely miss payment windows because municipal offices lack continuous, integrated digital mechanisms to cross-check assessment rolls against tax balances. While Fuentes and Salapa identify alarming delinquency trends across a six-year historical window, their study remains an exploratory retrospective analysis; they propose no software architecture, database triggers, or web-based tracking workflows. The proposed system directly resolves this operational vulnerability by engineering an integrated web platform that maintains real-time synchronization between assessor valuation records and treasury collection ledgers, eliminating reliance on manual counter reconciliations.

  - _Results & Independent Samples T-Test (pp. 4517–4518):_ Establishes a statistically significant variance ($t = -3.674, p = 0.021$) between manual intervention periods and collection yields, recommending automated digital tracking tools to stabilize municipal revenue.
