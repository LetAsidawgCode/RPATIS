# Revenue generation, collection efficiency, and fiscal sustainability: Evidence from a Philippine municipality

**Category:** [[Related Study local C2]]

**Glorina C. Damong. 2026. Revenue generation, collection efficiency, and fiscal sustainability: Evidence from a Philippine municipality. Public and Municipal Finance 15, 1 (January 2026), 14–26. https://doi.org/10.21511/pmf.15(1).2026.02**

- **Summary:** Through a ten-year longitudinal analysis of municipal fiscal records (2014–2023) paired with surveys of 325 taxpayers and revenue officers in La Trinidad, Benguet, Damong (2026) revealed that despite collection efficiency exceeding statutory targets (peaking at 173% in 2021), municipal tax operations remain constrained by manual delinquency tracking and paper-bound inter-office coordination.
 
- **Relevance:** This empirical study justifies the operational necessity of the Administrative Workflow Panel within the proposed system's comparative evaluation across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando. Demonstrating that hitting revenue targets often masks severe operational inefficiencies, Damong’s findings directly inform the automated delinquency flags, scheduled ledger reconciliation routines, and synchronized relational tables built into the Tax Payment Synchronization Module. Target fulfillment obscures administrative waste. In Philippine municipal halls, treasurers manually audit paper assessment ledgers to detect delinquent parcels, spending hours reconciling accounts that could be matched automatically. While Damong provides valuable longitudinal fiscal metrics across a decade of municipal operations, her study remains an empirical financial review; she constructs no software prototypes, automated delinquency flags, or relational data pipelines. The proposed system directly resolves this operational limitation by deploying an integrated web platform that automatically synchronizes tax declarations with treasury collection ledgers, flagging payment delinquencies in real time without manual audit overhead.

- **Source Reference(s):**

  - _Section 4: Challenges to Revenue Strategy Implementation (pp. 21–24):_ Identifies that reliance on manual delinquency tracking and uncoordinated inter-office notifications severely constrains municipal revenue sustainability.
