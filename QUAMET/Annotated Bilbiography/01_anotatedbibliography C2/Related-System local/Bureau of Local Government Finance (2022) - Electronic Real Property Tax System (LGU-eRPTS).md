# Electronic Real Property Tax System (LGU-eRPTS): Property Assessment and Municipal Revenue Administration System

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**Bureau of Local Government Finance. 2022. Electronic Real Property Tax System (LGU-eRPTS): Property Assessment and Municipal Revenue Administration System. Software application. Department of Finance, Manila, Philippines. https://blgf.gov.ph/**

- **Summary:** Commissioned by the Bureau of Local Government Finance to standardize municipal revenue governance, LGU-eRPTS provides local government units with localized software modules to compute real property appraisal values, generate computerized assessment rolls, and record treasury tax collections.

- **Relevance:** As the primary operational baseline evaluated in the study's comparative framework across Pasacao, Canaman, Magarao, and San Fernando, this system confirms that computerizing appraisal calculations and assessment rolls establishes necessary valuation consistency for municipal revenue operations. However, field observations in the benchmark municipality of Pasacao reveal severe structural limitations in localized eRPTS deployments: the software operates as isolated departmental database installations where assessment updates do not dynamically link to treasury ledgers. Processing a Transfer of Assessment still exceeds seven days because personnel must rely on physical paper endorsements, manual counter verification, and hand-carried clearance slips between offices. The system provides no document tracking queues, provincial escalation monitors, or automated reconciliation pipelines between the Assessor's and Treasurer's offices. The proposed system directly resolves these operational delays by deploying an integrated web platform that replaces disconnected departmental installations with an automated routing engine, real-time status tracking dashboards, and synchronized valuation-collection ledgers that eliminate physical paper handoffs and cut multi-day processing latency.
