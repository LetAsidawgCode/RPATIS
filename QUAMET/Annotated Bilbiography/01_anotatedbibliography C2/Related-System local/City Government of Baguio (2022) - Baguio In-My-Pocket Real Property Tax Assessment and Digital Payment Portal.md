# Baguio In-My-Pocket: Real Property Tax Assessment and Digital Payment Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**City Government of Baguio. 2022. Baguio In-My-Pocket: Real Property Tax Assessment and Digital Payment Portal. Mobile and web application. City Government of Baguio, Baguio City, Philippines. https://baguioinmypocket.ph/**

- **Summary:** Deployed by the City Government of Baguio as an integrated e-governance platform, Baguio In-My-Pocket enables registered citizens to access electronic real property tax assessments, compute quarterly dues, and execute digital payments through integrated third-party financial channels.

- **Relevance:** This metropolitan municipal application demonstrates that digital and mobile assessment retrieval substantially reduces queuing at treasury collection windows and simplifies billing delivery. However, Baguio In-My-Pocket utilizes an isolated account-to-document architecture detached from the internal operational workflows of the City Assessor's Office. When property records cannot be matched automatically—or when parcels undergo boundary realignment, reclassification, or ownership transfers—the application cannot route requests dynamically or track internal appraisal progress. Instead, taxpayers are instructed to secure manual in-person certifications from the City Assessor and physically carry hard-copy clearance slips to the City Treasury. The proposed system directly overcomes this operational barrier by integrating an Assessment Request Routing Module with automated status tracking dashboards, enabling personnel from both municipal offices to route valuation updates and track document workflows electronically without counter escalations.
