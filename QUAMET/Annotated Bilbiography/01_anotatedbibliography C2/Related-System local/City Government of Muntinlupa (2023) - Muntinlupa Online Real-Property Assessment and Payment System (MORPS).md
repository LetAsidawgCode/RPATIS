# Muntinlupa Online Real-Property Assessment and Payment System (MORPS)

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**City Government of Muntinlupa. 2023. Muntinlupa Online Real-Property Assessment and Payment System (MORPS). Web application. City Government of Muntinlupa, Muntinlupa City, Philippines. https://rpt.muntinlupacity.gov.ph/**

- **Summary:** Developed by the City Government of Muntinlupa, MORPS functions as an online municipal portal that allows registered real property owners to query tax declaration balances, calculate quarterly real property tax liabilities, and execute payments through electronic payment gateways.

- **Relevance:** This metropolitan municipal platform demonstrates that self-service web portals streamline citizen access to valuation records using Property Index Numbers (PIN) and Tax Declaration (TD) numbers, substantially reducing counter congestion. However, MORPS functions strictly as a treasury-managed payment interface that depends on static, pre-cleared assessment rolls. It remains functionally isolated from the internal appraisal workflows of the City Assessor's Office. When property owners need to process transfers of assessment, correct erroneous valuation classifications, or settle historical ledger discrepancies, the portal offers no tracking capabilities; taxpayers must still physically visit the local assessor to process paper endorsements and manually route documents between departments. The proposed system directly overcomes this operational silo by deploying an Assessment Request Routing Module and an Administrative Workflow Panel, enabling municipal assessor and treasury personnel to track document statuses in real time and synchronize valuation revisions with payment ledgers without requiring in-person counter appointments.

- **Source Reference(s):**

  - _Muntinlupa City Revenue Operations Manual:_ Highlights procedures for on-site assessment reconciliation and counter validation required for manual property transfers.
