# Enhanced Tax Revenue and Collection System (ETRACS): Real Property Assessment and Tax Administration Module

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**Rameses Systems Inc. 2022. Enhanced Tax Revenue and Collection System (ETRACS): Real Property Assessment and Tax Administration Module. Web and client-server software application. Rameses Systems Inc., Cebu City, Philippines. http://etracs.org**

- **Summary:** Built upon the proprietary Osiris 2 Enterprise Framework with a Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) and relational databases (MySQL/MS SQL Server), ETRACS digitizes municipal revenue administration by interconnecting Field Appraisal and Assessment Sheet (FAAS) encoding, computerized Tax Declaration (TD) generation, and treasury tax collection workflows.

- **Relevance:** This enterprise platform validates the core operational model of the proposed system by demonstrating that directly connecting municipal assessor valuation records to treasury collection ledgers prevents billing discrepancies and manual reconciliation errors. However, ETRACS operates as an installed enterprise suite requiring dedicated workstation clients and complex local server infrastructure, creating substantial deployment and maintenance barriers for resource-constrained local governments. The platform contains no workflow-tracking monitor to trace physical paper movement or external multi-office approvals routed between municipal offices and provincial authorities, such as the Provincial Capitol in Pili. Migrating decades-old manual paper records into ETRACS without a localized needs assessment framework routinely produces severe backlogs and computation mismatches. The proposed system directly resolves these operational constraints by deploying a lightweight, browser-based synchronization platform with an Administrative Workflow Panel and an Assessment Request Routing Module; this design automates document tracking, logs provincial approval latency, and establishes an empirical needs assessment framework to reconcile legacy records without heavy enterprise overhead.
