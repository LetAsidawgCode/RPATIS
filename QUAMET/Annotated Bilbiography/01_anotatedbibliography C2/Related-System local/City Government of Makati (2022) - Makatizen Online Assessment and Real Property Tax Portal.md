# Makatizen Online Assessment and Real Property Tax Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**City Government of Makati. 2022. Makatizen Online Assessment and Real Property Tax Portal. Web application. City Government of Makati, Makati City, Philippines. https://makationlinepayments.com/**

- **Summary:** Deployed by the City Government of Makati, this web-based municipal tax portal enables real property owners to query assessment balances using Tax Declaration Numbers, review computed quarterly tax dues, and execute payments through integrated digital payment gateways.

- **Relevance:** This metropolitan tax portal demonstrates how online inquiry mechanisms allow taxpayers to verify valuation records and compute tax liabilities remotely. However, the Makatizen portal operates under strict operational constraints: it functions strictly on pre-cleared, non-delinquent property records and remains disconnected from the City Assessor's internal valuation workflow. When a property parcel involves delinquent accounts, reassessment disputes, boundary adjustments, or multi-step ownership transfers, the portal locks out electronic processing and requires property owners to physically visit the Realty Tax Division and the City Assessor to route paper clearances. The platform provides no automated inter-office communication pipeline or document tracking dashboard to monitor pending assessment revisions between departments. The proposed system directly overcomes these operational boundaries by engineering an Assessment Request Routing Module and an inter-office synchronization engine, ensuring that valuation revisions, delinquent account verifications, and property transfers are tracked collaboratively by municipal assessor and treasury staff without requiring in-person paper routing.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Makatizen Online Payments Service Manual:_ Details online search of real property tax assessments via Tax Declaration Numbers.
  - _Makati Assessment and Collection Workflow Guidelines:_ Specifies administrative restrictions on delinquent property accounts and the operational separation of valuation update workflows.
