# Antipolo Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**City Government of Antipolo. 2022. Antipolo Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal. Web application. City Government of Antipolo, Antipolo City, Philippines. https://online.antipolo.ph/**

- **Summary:** Implemented by the City Government of Antipolo, this web-based municipal tax portal enables property owners to enroll Tax Declaration Numbers, calculate real property tax liabilities across quarterly periods, and complete payments via integrated digital payment channels.

- **Relevance:** This local government platform confirms that self-service billing calculation accelerates transaction completion for standardized, active property accounts. Despite these front-facing collection gains, the portal operates strictly as an isolated treasury-side calculator. It requires prior manual verification of historical official receipts and provides no dynamic workflow tracking for accounts undergoing property transfers, building re-assessments, or provincial tax mapping reviews with the City Assessor's Office. Complex assessment workflows remain trapped in paper handoffs, requiring taxpayers to book in-person appointments to reconcile discrepancies. The proposed system resolves these back-office coordination gaps by engineering an Administrative Workflow Panel and a Tax Payment Synchronization Module that link valuation adjustments directly to treasury ledgers, providing real-time document tracking across departmental handoffs without requiring manual in-person verification.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _Antipolo Online Portal User Guide:_ Details user registration, property enrollment via Tax Declaration Numbers, and online tax computation.
  - _City Treasurer's Office Appointment Guidelines:_ Specifies the requirement for taxpayers to secure in-person appointments for complex assessment adjustments and manual receipt verifications.
