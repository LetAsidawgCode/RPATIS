# Iloilo City Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal

**Category:** [[Related System local C2]]

**City Government of Iloilo. 2023. Iloilo City Online Real Property Tax Assessment and Payment Portal. Web application. City Government of Iloilo, Iloilo City, Philippines. https://iloilocity.gov.ph/**

- **Summary:** Developed in-house by the Project Development and Management Team and managed by the City Treasurer's Office, this municipal portal enables registered property owners to create user profiles, generate digital Real Property Tax Orders of Payment, and settle tax liabilities electronically.

- **Relevance:** This local government system demonstrates that automating billing retrieval and generating digital tax orders of payment substantially reduces counter congestion in municipal revenue offices. However, the portal functions as an isolated front-facing billing inquiry tool that relies exclusively on pre-compiled, static assessment rolls from the City Assessor's Office. It contains no bidirectional synchronization or internal document tracking mechanisms. If an assessment is undergoing a transfer of ownership, parcel consolidation, or valuation protest, the portal cannot reflect in-progress appraisal updates or track the movement of physical approval sheets between the assessor and treasurer. Municipal personnel must still perform manual ledger cross-checks to verify whether tax clearances correspond to current property boundaries. The proposed system directly resolves this operational gap by incorporating a centralized Assessment Request Routing Module and an Administrative Workflow Panel, allowing assessor and treasurer personnel to track document workflows end-to-end and automate valuation-to-collection ledger reconciliation in real time.
