# South Australian Integrated Land Information System (SAILIS)

**Category:** [[Related System international C2]]

**Government of South Australia. 2022. South Australian Integrated Land Information System (SAILIS). Web application. Land Services SA and Department for Infrastructure and Transport, Adelaide, SA, Australia. https://sailis.lssa.com.au/**

- **Summary:** Developed by the Government of South Australia, SAILIS provides public agencies, conveyancers, and citizens with electronic access to official land titles, digital valuation books, survey plans, and historic land transfer records.

- **Relevance:** This comprehensive state land information platform validates the feasibility of maintaining an authoritative single digital source of truth for parcel title histories, property transfers, and statutory valuations across public administrative bodies. Nevertheless, SAILIS functions primarily as an external title search platform that charges transactional access fees and requires specialized conveyancing intermediaries; it offers no lightweight administrative workflow tools for small municipal councils to track day-to-day tax clearance approvals or resolve local assessment processing backlogs. The proposed system bridges this operational divide by implementing an internal, web-based administrative workflow panel that tracks assessment document statuses and synchronizes records directly between municipal assessor and treasurer staff at zero transactional cost.
