# City of Cape Town General Valuation Roll and Property Rates Portal

**Category:** [[Related System international C2]]

**City of Cape Town. 2023. City of Cape Town General Valuation Roll and Property Rates Portal. Web application. City of Cape Town Metropolitan Municipality, Cape Town, South Africa. https://web1.capetown.gov.za/web1/gv2022/**

- **Summary:** The City of Cape Town deployed this municipal web platform to publish certified general property valuation rolls, provide online municipal property rate estimators, and process electronic valuation objection submissions for metropolitan property owners.

- **Relevance:** This municipal platform proves that publishing certified valuation rolls and automated rating estimators enhances public transparency and substantially reduces routine counter inquiries during periodic municipal reassessments. However, the portal is designed strictly to publish static, four-year general valuation rolls; it offers no workflow tracking mechanism to trace ongoing physical building inspection approvals, supplemental assessment routing, or inter-departmental clearances between valuation officers and revenue accounting teams. When new structures are erected or boundary subdivisions occur mid-cycle, staff must manage approvals through unmonitored paper routing. The proposed system addresses these operational boundaries by implementing real-time assessment request routing, active document tracking monitors, and continuous data synchronization that eliminate multi-day approval delays between municipal assessors and treasurers.
