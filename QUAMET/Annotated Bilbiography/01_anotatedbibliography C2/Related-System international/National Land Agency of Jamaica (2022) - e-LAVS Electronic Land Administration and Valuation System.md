# e-LAVS: Electronic Land Administration and Valuation System

**Category:** [[Related System international C2]]

**National Land Agency of Jamaica. 2022. e-LAVS: Electronic Land Administration and Valuation System. Web application. National Land Agency, Kingston, Jamaica. https://www.nla.gov.jm/**

- **Summary:** Operated by the National Land Agency of Jamaica, e-LAVS combines GIS parcel mapping, statutory property valuation rolls, and automated Property Tax Certificate generation within a nationwide web portal.

- **Relevance:** This public sector e-governance platform provides a valuable precedent for modernizing property valuation and land taxation in a developing-nation context transitioning away from manual record books. However, e-LAVS operates primarily at the national agency level and experiences substantial data synchronization lags when routing valuation revisions to localized parish tax collection offices, where local revenue staff still face manual record reconciliation delays. The platform lacks real-time, event-triggered document tracking to notify parish offices when appraisals or ownership records are modified. The proposed system directly resolves this inter-office latency by prioritizing local municipal-level integration, establishing an automated document routing and tax payment synchronization pipeline between the Municipal Assessor's Office and the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
