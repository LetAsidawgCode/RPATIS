# Search Property Information Service and Digital Land Register

**Category:** [[Related System international C2]]

**HM Land Registry. 2022. Search Property Information Service and Digital Land Register. Web application. Her Majesty's Land Registry, London, UK. https://www.gov.uk/search-property-information-land-registry**

- **Summary:** Deployed by HM Land Registry as the official digital register for England and Wales, this web-based service allows property owners, conveyancers, and local rating authorities to inspect electronic land titles, registered parcel boundaries, and historical ownership records.

- **Relevance:** This national digital platform proves that establishing an authoritative electronic registry of property ownership records curbs fraudulent transactions and minimizes manual title verification across public offices. However, the system operates strictly as an external title recording repository that remains structurally detached from local council valuation rolls and municipal tax collection databases. Local billing authorities cannot trace in-progress property transfers or automatically synchronize boundary alterations with local rating ledgers; instead, municipal clerks must manually query the registry to reconcile property ownership changes. The proposed system directly resolves this operational boundary by engineering an integrated assessment tracking platform that automates document routing and directly links property ownership transfer workflows to local treasury tax collection ledgers in real time.

- **Source Reference(s):**
  - _HM Land Registry Digital Search Guidelines:_ Outlines online title register searches, title plan downloads, and registered ownership verification procedures.
  - _UK Local Taxation and Land Registration Integration Framework:_ Discusses the administrative separation between central land registration and local government tax roll maintenance.
